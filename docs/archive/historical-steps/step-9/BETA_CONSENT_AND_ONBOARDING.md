## 1. Welcome to the FoodCue Closed Beta

Thank you for participating in the FoodCue Closed Beta programme. 

FoodCue’s purpose is to help households:
1. **Know your food:** Understand packaged food labels, ingredient lists, additive codes, and allergen declarations.
2. **Manage your kitchen:** Track what food items you have at home, monitor expiry dates, and minimize household waste.

---

## 2. Important Disclaimers & What FoodCue Is NOT

Before using the app, please understand these non-negotiable boundaries:
- **FoodCue does not diagnose, treat, or cure allergies or health conditions.**
- **FoodCue does not certify whether a product is 100% safe for medical conditions (such as severe anaphylactic peanut allergies or celiac disease).**
- **Label information may change across manufacturing batches.** Always inspect the physical packaging text before consuming any food product.
- **When FoodCue displays an amber "Uncertain" notice**, it means camera lighting, packaging curvature, or text smudging prevented clear verification. You must inspect the package yourself.

---

## 3. Privacy & Data Handling Commitment

We treat your household privacy with the highest respect:
- **Local-Only by Default:** Your food photos, scanned ingredients, pantry inventory, and shopping lists are saved directly inside your phone's browser storage. They are **not** sent to any remote server or company database.
- **Zero Third-Party Trackers:** FoodCue does not use advertising trackers, Google Analytics, or third-party tracking cookies.
- **Right to Erase:** You can delete all your stored data at any moment by navigating to **Settings -> Clear All Local Data**.

## 5. Step-by-Step Onboarding Instructions

1. **Launch FoodCue:** Open your mobile browser and navigate to the private beta link: `https://food-cue.vercel.app`.
2. **Add to Home Screen:** Tap your browser menu and select **"Add to Home Screen"** to use FoodCue like an app.
3. **Try Your First Scan:**
   - Tap **SmartScan** in the bottom navigation bar.
   - Hold your camera over any barcode or nutritional label.
   - Review the candidate match.
   - Tap **"Confirm Label"** after verifying the physical box.
4. **Add to Kitchen:** After confirming a label, tap **"Add to Pantry"** to set quantity and expiry date.
5. **Report an Issue:** If you spot an unreadable word, incorrect additive, or app freeze, send a screenshot to the founder beta support channel.
