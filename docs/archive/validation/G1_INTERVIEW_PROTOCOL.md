# FoodCue Validation Protocol — Gate G1 Qualitative User Interview
**Gate:** G1 — Problem & Household Need Validation  
**Protocol Code:** PROT-VAL-G1-QUAL-01  
**Authoritative Version:** 1.0  
**Effective Date:** 20 October 2026 – 10 November 2026  
**Target Geography:** Urban India (Delhi NCR, Mumbai MMR, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Pune)  

---

## 1. Research Objectives

1. Determine whether urban Indian middle-income and upper-middle-income households experience measurable pain points in:
   - Packaged food transparency (hidden allergens, palm oil, refined sugars, artificial additives).
   - Food storage awareness (cabinet blindness, duplicate purchasing, forgotten perishables).
   - Expiry and shelf-life tracking (expired spices, rancid oils, stale snacks).
2. Quantify user willingness to scan or log packaging barcode / labels at the point of grocery unboxing.
3. Validate user attitudes toward privacy, local storage versus mandatory account creation, and offline accessibility.

---

## 2. Participant Recruitment & Sampling Parameters

### 2.1 Screening Criteria
- Primary or co-decision maker for grocery purchasing and kitchen stocking in the household.
- Purchases packaged food groceries at least twice a month (via quick-commerce e.g. Blinkit/Zepto/Instamart, modern retail e.g. DMart/Nature's Basket, or local kirana).
- Resident of Tier 1 or Tier 2 urban agglomerations.
- Smartphone user (Android or iOS).

### 2.2 Sample Size & Stratification
- Target completed interviews: **$N = 30$**
- Geographic distribution:
  - Delhi NCR: 7 households (23.3%)
  - Mumbai MMR: 7 households (23.3%)
  - Bengaluru: 7 households (23.3%)
  - Hyderabad: 5 households (16.7%)
  - Pune: 4 households (13.3%)
- Demographic mix:
  - 10 Young working couples / flatmates (22–32 yrs)
  - 12 Nuclear families with children (28–45 yrs)
  - 8 Multi-generational households (35–60 yrs)

---

## 3. Semi-Structured Interview Schedule (45 Minutes)

### Phase 1: Context & Grocery Ingestion Routine (10 mins)
- *Q1.1:* Walk me through what happens when groceries arrive at your home from Blinkit/Zepto or after a supermarket run. Who unboxes them, and where do items go?
- *Q1.2:* How do you currently remember what you already have in your pantry, fridge, or kitchen cupboards before ordering new supplies?

### Phase 2: Kitchen Blindness, Duplication & Food Waste (12 mins)
- *Q2.1:* In the past month, have you found items pushed to the back of the shelf that expired or went stale before you finished them? What were they?
- *Q2.2:* Have you ever bought a condiment, spice box, or snack only to discover you already had an unopened pack at home? How often does this happen?

### Phase 3: Packaging Literacy, FSSAI Labels & Allergens (13 mins)
- *Q3.1:* When you pick up a packaged food item in India, do you read the ingredients list or nutrition table? Why or why not?
- *Q3.2:* What frustrates you most about packaged food labels in India (e.g. font size, technical jargon, misleading "healthy" claims)?
- *Q3.3:* Does anyone in your family suffer from food allergies, intolerances, or specific dietary restrictions (e.g. lactose, gluten, nuts, strictly vegetarian)? How do you verify safety?

### Phase 4: Workflow Acceptance & Privacy Expectations (10 mins)
- *Q4.1:* If an app allowed you to scan barcodes and read pack labels in under 2 seconds completely offline, would you take 60 seconds to scan items during unboxing?
- *Q4.2:* How do you feel about kitchen apps requiring phone number logins or cloud uploads of your grocery habits? Would you prefer your kitchen inventory to stay private on your phone?

---

## 4. Data Capture, Coding & Quality Controls

1. All interview responses recorded with explicit verbal consent, anonymized under participant IDs (`INT-01` through `INT-30`).
2. Transcripts coded under 5 thematic nodes:
   - `P1_KITCHEN_BLINDNESS`: Inability to recall stock without visual inspection.
   - `P2_DUPLICATE_BUYING`: Financial loss from redundant purchases.
   - `P3_LABEL_OPAQUENESS`: Incomprehensible or deceptive nutritional labelling.
   - `P4_ALLERGEN_ANXIETY`: Fear of hidden allergens or formulation changes.
   - `T1_PRIVACY_SENSITIVITY`: Distrust of mandatory cloud logins for personal household data.
3. Inter-coder agreement target: Cohen's Kappa $\kappa \ge 0.85$.
