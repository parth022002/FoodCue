Every product in this list was physically purchased, photographed across all four panels (Front, Ingredients, Allergens, Nutrition/Batch), and verified against the Food Safety and Standards (Labelling and Display) Regulations, 2020.

## 3. Data Integrity & Reproducibility Verification

1. All 100 items are permanently stored in FoodCue's test assets and can be queried or benchmarked using automated integration scripts.
2. The dataset provides $100\%$ test coverage over all FSSAI statutory mandatory allergen declarations:
   - Cereals containing gluten
   - Crustaceans and products thereof
   - Eggs and egg products
   - Fish and fish products
   - Peanuts, soybeans and products thereof
   - Milk and milk products
   - Tree nuts and their products
   - Sulphites in concentrations of 10mg/kg or more
