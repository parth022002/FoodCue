## 2. Weekly Review Protocol (Weeks 1 to 4)

### Week 1: Activation, Scan Usability & First Impressions
- **Review Date Window:** Days 1 to 7 post-onboarding.
- **Key Questions:**
  1. Did the household successfully scan at least 3 packaged food items?
  2. Was the camera viewfinder responsive and intuitive?
  3. Did the participant understand why they had to press "Confirm Label" before seeing food details?
  4. Were there any lighting or camera focus issues?

### Week 2: Kitchen Pantry Usage & Expiry Reminders
- **Review Date Window:** Days 8 to 14.
- **Key Questions:**
  1. Did the household add items to "My Kitchen"?
  2. Was the distinction between Expiration Date (strict safety) and Best-Before Date (freshness) clear?
  3. Did the household use the Shopping List feature?

### Week 3: Stress Testing, Challenging Packs & Offline Use
- **Review Date Window:** Days 15 to 21.
- **Key Questions:**
  1. Did the participant test wrinkled packets, curved soda cans, or foil pouches?
  2. Did the participant notice how the app behaved offline?
  3. Were amber "Uncertain / Unreadable" notices encountered and correctly interpreted?

### Week 4: Final Evaluation & Retention Benchmark
- **Review Date Window:** Days 22 to 28.
- **Key Questions:**
  1. Did the household continue using FoodCue in week 4 without prompting?
  2. Target: At least **30% retention** of activated households.
  3. What is the single biggest barrier preventing daily kitchen use?
